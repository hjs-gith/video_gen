# AI Jargon Curriculum — Cluster Edition

A cluster-based curriculum for a video class teaching AI jargon to non-developers. Each cluster is a **mini-season** — a thematic arc that teaches related concepts in order, building on each previous episode.

**Total: 50 terms · 9 clusters · ~33 episodes**

Tier remains as a **difficulty badge** on each term (T1–T3), used for thumbnails and viewer signaling, but no longer drives episode order.

---

## How to Read This Doc

- **Clusters** = mini-seasons. Released in sequence.
- **Episode order within a cluster** = narrative arc, each builds on the last.
- **Tier badge (T1/T2/T2.5/T2.7/T3)** = difficulty signal, shown on thumbnail.
- **Some episodes pair 2 closely related terms** when one alone is too thin for 60 sec.

## Release Strategy

**Cluster-by-cluster as mini-seasons.** Each cluster is one short "season":

1. **Season 1: Meet the AI** (5 episodes) — drop weekly or all at once
2. **Season 2: How You Talk to AI** (5 episodes)
3. **Season 3: What AI Remembers & Knows** (5 episodes)
4. **Season 4: When AI Gets Things Wrong** (5 episodes)
5. **Season 5: What AI Can Actually Do** (6 episodes)
6. **Season 6: Under the Hood** (6 episodes)
7. **Season 7: The Control Panel** (5 episodes)
8. **Season 8: The Big Picture** (5 episodes)

Each season ends with a recap or quiz episode (optional). Between seasons, you can take a production break, run a viewer Q&A, or post a "cluster connection" recap.

## Tier Badge System (for Thumbnails)

Even though clusters drive order, the tier badge stays visible to viewers:

| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **T1** | Everyday term — you've definitely heard it |
| **T2** | Workplace term — likely heard in meetings |
| **T2.5** | Capability term — newer, trending |
| **T2.7** | Technical knob — for hands-on users |
| **T3** | Big-picture term — thought-leadership level |

Tier dictates only the badge color/label on thumbnail; cluster dictates episode order.
